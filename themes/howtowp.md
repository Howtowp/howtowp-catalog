## About

**HowToWP** is a theme in the [How to WP](https://howtowp.ir) collection.

HowToWP theme and plugin store theme, with WooCommerce support and a custom single-product page.

- **Type:** Theme
- **Category:** Theme & store
- **Version:** 1.0.3

## Features

- A theme for a theme-and-plugin store, with WooCommerce support.
- A custom single-product layout, tabs, a gallery lightbox, and preview video.
- A purchase box that changes with the visitor’s state (guest, no subscription, club member, member without a club plan).
- Active club members can download for free and place a zero-amount order.
- Product Q&A and feature requests, plus Product and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD.
- Persian numerals and prices.

## Requirements

- **WordPress:** 6.0+
- **PHP:** 7.4+
