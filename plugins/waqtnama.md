## About

**وقت‌نما — زمانبندی تحویل و خدمات ووکامرس** is a plugin in the [How to WP](https://howtowp.ir) collection.

WooCommerce delivery and service scheduling: service types, technicians, time slots, geography, and checkout.

- **Type:** Plugin
- **Category:** Iranian payments & shipping
- **Version:** 0.3.2

## Features

- Schedule delivery, installation, and services, and attach the booking to the WooCommerce cart and checkout.
- Service types, capacity-limited time slots, Jalali/Gregorian holidays, and geographic rules.
- Time-slot selection on classic checkout and Checkout Blocks.
- Technician management, daily capacity, service areas, and an admin calendar.
- Technician-panel and review-form shortcodes.
- Optional integrations with SMS, Dokan, WooCommerce Subscriptions, and Tapin.

## Requirements

- **WordPress:** 6.0+

- **WooCommerce:** 7.0+
