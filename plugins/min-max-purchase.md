## About

**Min Max Purchase Control** is a plugin in the [How to WP](https://howtowp.ir) collection.

Minimum and maximum purchase quantities and purchase increments for WooCommerce products, with per-user and default settings.

- **Type:** Plugin
- **Category:** WooCommerce & sales
- **Version:** 1.0.0

## Features

- Set minimum and maximum purchase quantities per product.
- Purchase increments (for example, packs of five).
- Setting priority: product, then user, then global defaults.
- Cart validation and Elementor compatibility.
- Bulk editing of settings for products and users.

- The plugin header does not declare a separate minimum version.
