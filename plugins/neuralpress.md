## About

**NeuralPress** is a plugin in the [How to WP](https://howtowp.ir) collection.

Prepare your WordPress site for AI engines — GEO/AEO signals, crawler control, and verified bot analytics.

- **Type:** Plugin
- **Category:** SEO & tools
- **Version:** 2.0.0

## Features

- Prepare a WordPress site for AI engines (GEO/AEO signals).
- Outputs such as Schema.org JSON-LD, Open Graph, llms.txt, llm-data.json, sitemap optimization, and IndexNow.
- Per-crawler robots.txt access control with allow/deny lists.
- Verified AI-bot visit logging and analytics.
- Event webhooks and a key-authenticated REST API.

## Requirements

- **WordPress:** 6.5+
- **PHP:** 8.1+
