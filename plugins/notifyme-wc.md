## About

**NotifyMe - اطلاع از موجودی ووکامرس** is a plugin in the [How to WP](https://howtowp.ir) collection.

A back-in-stock button for out-of-stock WooCommerce products, with notifications via Bale, Telegram, and SMS (proxy and reCAPTCHA supported).

- **Type:** Plugin
- **Category:** WooCommerce & sales
- **Version:** 1.0.0

## Features

- A “notify me when back in stock” button for out-of-stock products.
- Alerts via Bale, Telegram, and SMS (Kavenegar or Ghasedak).
- Proxy support (HTTP, HTTPS, SOCKS4, SOCKS5) and an optional Google Apps Script relay.
- Google reCAPTCHA v3 to reduce spam.
- A button shortcode for the product page or a specific product ID.

## Requirements

- **WordPress:** 6.0+
- **PHP:** 7.4+
- **WooCommerce:** 8.0+
