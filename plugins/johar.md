## About

**جوهر (Johar)** is a plugin in the [How to WP](https://howtowp.ir) collection.

Payment-link and invoice management for Iranian sellers.

- **Type:** Plugin
- **Category:** Iranian payments & shipping
- **Version:** 1.7.1

## Features

- Payment links and invoice management for Iranian sellers.
- ZarinPal and Zibal gateways, plus card-to-card payment with manual confirmation.
- Send payment links via SMS, Telegram, or Bale.
- Reminders for abandoned payments.
- Optional WooCommerce integration.

## Requirements

- **WordPress:** 6.0+
- **PHP:** 8.1+
