## About

**Dr 404** is a plugin in the [How to WP](https://howtowp.ir) collection.

Professional 404-page templates and maintenance mode for WordPress, enabled in one click.

- **Type:** Plugin
- **Category:** SEO & tools
- **Version:** 1.0.0

## Features

- Ready-made 404 and maintenance templates, enabled in one click.
- Eighteen 404 templates across several styles and six maintenance templates (including Coming Soon and a countdown).
- Visitors see the maintenance page while administrators keep full access.
- A 404 stats dashboard and SEO settings for the error page.
- Persian RTL design, without replacing your active theme.

## Requirements

- **WordPress:** 5.8+
- **PHP:** 7.4+
