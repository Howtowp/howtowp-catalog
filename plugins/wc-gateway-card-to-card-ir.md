## About

**WooCommerce Gateway Card To Card IR** is a plugin in the [How to WP](https://howtowp.ir) collection.

A card-to-card payment gateway for WooCommerce (Iran Shetab network) with a custom payment page, card listing, and receipt upload.

- **Type:** Plugin
- **Category:** Iranian payments & shipping
- **Version:** 1.3.3

## Features

- A card-to-card gateway for Iran’s Shetab network.
- Shows bank cards on checkout with a copy button and readable formatting.
- Manage multiple cards in the admin (number, bank, account holder, IBAN).
- Optional tracking-code field and receipt-image upload.
- Receipt preview on the order screen and a receipt column in the orders list.

## Requirements

- **WordPress:** 5.8+
- **PHP:** 7.4+
- **WooCommerce:** 5.0+
