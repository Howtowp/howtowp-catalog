## About

**CardQuest for WooCommerce** is a plugin in the [How to WP](https://howtowp.ir) collection.

Gamification for WooCommerce stores — every interaction earns a card; complete sets for rewards.

- **Type:** Plugin
- **Category:** Marketing & loyalty
- **Version:** 1.0.0

## Features

- Store gamification: interactions earn cards, and completed sets grant rewards.
- WooCommerce HPOS compatible.
- Admin dashboard with stats and reports.
- Storefront card-album shortcode.
- Reward engine (including coupons and shipping) plus admin management of cards, sets, and events.

## Requirements

- **WordPress:** 6.3+
- **PHP:** 8.0+
- **WooCommerce:** 8.5+
