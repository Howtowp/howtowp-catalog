## About

**LoyaltyPlus** is a plugin in the [How to WP](https://howtowp.ir) collection.

WooCommerce loyalty, tasks, referral, spin, and leaderboards.

- **Type:** Plugin
- **Category:** Marketing & loyalty
- **Version:** 1.0.0

## Features

- WooCommerce loyalty points, tasks, referrals, a spin wheel, and leaderboards.
- Shortcodes for the dashboard, level, history, tasks, rewards, leaderboard, spin wheel, challenges, and balance.
- A submissions queue for task proofs, plus campaigns, levels, and a points ledger.
- Reports, CSV export/import, and configurable notification templates.
- Telegram webhook support; Bale task proofs are manually reviewed in version 1.0.

## Requirements

- **WordPress:** 5.9+
- **PHP:** 7.4+
