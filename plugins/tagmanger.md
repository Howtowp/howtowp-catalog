## About

**TagManger** is a plugin in the [How to WP](https://howtowp.ir) collection.

Full conversion tracking for lead-gen WordPress sites — forms, phone, email, and messengers to GA4.

- **Type:** Plugin
- **Category:** SEO & tools
- **Version:** 1.0.3

## Features

- Lead-gen conversion tracking routed to GA4 / the dataLayer.
- Tracks Gravity Forms, Elementor Forms, tel: and mailto: links, WhatsApp, and Telegram.
- No raw PII is pushed to the dataLayer.
- Optional Google Ads lead integration, as documented by the plugin.

## Requirements

- **WordPress:** 6.0+
- **PHP:** 8.1+
